Tom Petty

Thomas Earl Petty (October 20, 1950 – October 2, 2017) was an American singer, songwriter, and guitarist. He was the leader and frontman of the rock bands Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers and Mudcrutch.

Tom Petty was born in Gainesville, FL in 1950, and the city still considers him to be its "Number 1 Son." Following his death, the Florida Gators football team began playing his hit song, I Won't Back Down in the break between the third and fourth quarters at home games, a tradition that continues to this day.

Murals dedicated to Tom Petty are scattered throughout the city, and in Duckpond, the neighborhood he grew up in, the local park was renamed in his honor.
